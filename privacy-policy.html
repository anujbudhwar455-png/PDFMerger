Privacy Policy

PDF Merger (Android) · Desrein Studios · Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Desrein Studios (“we”, “us”) handles information in the PDF Merger mobile application (package com.aistudio.pdfmerger.ajxbv) developed by Anuj Budhwar.

1. On-device PDF processing

PDF Merger is an offline-first utility. Merge, split, and related PDF operations run locally on your device. We do not upload, sell, or transmit the contents of your PDF documents to Desrein Studios servers. We do not claim ownership of your documents.

2. Local history and storage

The app may store merge/split history and temporary working files on your device (for example, using a local database and app storage). This data stays on the device. Uninstalling the app typically removes local app data. We do not operate a cloud account for your PDF history.

3. Information we do not collect in-app

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name or email address inside the app. Contact via email is only if you choose to write to us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free tier may show ads served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob may process device and advertising identifiers (Advertising ID), IP address / network info, and interaction data as described in Google’s policies, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud/abuse.

  • AdMob application ID used by the app: ca-app-pub-9820225460126614~4700245601
  • Permission declared: com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID
  • Your PDF document contents are not uploaded for advertising.
  • Premium users (via in-app purchase) may see ads suppressed in the app.

See also: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional Premium unlocks use Google Play Billing (product id premium_upgrade). Google processes purchase tokens and related billing data under Google’s terms. We do not receive your full payment card details; payment is handled by Google Play.

6. Network permissions

The app uses INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for AdMob and Play Billing connectivity. Document import uses the system file picker / document access you grant when prompted; that access is for local processing, not for background advertising tracking of your files.

7. Data sharing and selling

  • We do not sell your PDF contents or personal information.
  • Third parties involved in ads/billing are Google (AdMob / Play), subject to their policies.
  • We do not sell or share PDF document contents with advertisers.

8. Security

Communication with Google Play and AdMob uses encrypted HTTPS. Local files are protected by your device’s storage and OS permissions. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

9. Children’s privacy

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your choices and deletion

  • Clear local history / uninstall the app to remove on-device app data.
  • Manage Advertising ID / ads preferences in Android system settings.
  • Manage or request refunds for purchases through Google Play.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law.
